Physics

A sizing estimate, not a certification. Material values are placeholders: use your supplier's datasheet, and apply AGMA or ISO 6336 factors for a final design.

Formulas used
  • Force along the rack F = T / r, where r is the pitch radius. Separating force Fr = F·tanα. Normal force Fn = F / cosα.
  • Load force F = m(a + g·sinθ + μg·cosθ) + external. Motor torque = F·r / (G·η).
  • Lewis bending stress σ = F·Kv / (b·m·Y), with Kv = (6.1 + v) / 6.1 and Y from the 20° form-factor table.
  • Hertz contact stress σH = √(Fn·E* / (π·b·r·sinα)), with E* = E / (2(1 − ν²)) for two equal materials.
  • Resolution = π·m·z / (steps × microsteps × G). Added backlash from mount error ≈ 2·Δc·tanα.
  • Contact ratio ε = [√(ra² − rb²) − r·sinα + m / sinα] / (π·m·cosα). Undercut limit z ≥ 2 / sin²α.
