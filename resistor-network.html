Equations used
  • Divider: Vout = Vin·R2/(R1 + R2). With a load, R2 is replaced by R2‖RL = R2·RL/(R2 + RL), so Vout = Vin·(R2‖RL)/(R1 + R2‖RL).
  • Thévenin equivalent seen by the load: Vth = Vin·R2/(R1 + R2), Rth = R1‖R2. The loaded output is Vth·RL/(Rth + RL).
  • Series: Req = ΣR, I = Vin/Req, each drop V = I·R. Parallel: 1/Req = Σ(1/R), each branch current I = Vin/R.
  • Power in each resistor: P = V·I = I²R = V²/R. Compare against the rating; leave a margin of at least 2×.
  • Tolerance: worst-case Vout comes from R1 high with R2 (and RL) low, and the reverse, each by ±tolerance.
  • Target Vout: Rbot = R1·Vt/(Vin − Vt) (or R1 = Rbot·(Vin − Vt)/Vt), where Rbot is R2 unloaded or R2‖RL when a load is fitted, then the nearest value in the chosen series. E12 and E24 are the standard lists; E96 is computed as 10^(n/96) rounded to three digits.
  • Loading rule of thumb: keep RL at least 10 × R2 to hold the error near 10%, 100 × R2 for about 1%, or buffer the tap with an op-amp follower.
