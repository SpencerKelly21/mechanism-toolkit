Physics

A sizing estimate, not a certification. Material and wear limits are placeholders: use your nut and screw supplier's datasheet. Ball screw life (L10) needs the dynamic load rating and is not included.

Formulas used
  • Pitch p = l / n. Mean diameter dm = d − p/2. Root diameter dr = d − p. Lead angle λ = atan(l / π·dm).
  • Torque to raise (drive against the load), with thread half-angle α: TR = (F·dm/2)·(l + π·μ·dm·secα) / (π·dm − μ·l·secα) + F·μc·dc/2. Ball screw: TR = F·l / (2π·η) + collar term.
  • Torque to lower: TL = (F·dm/2)·(π·μ·dm·secα − l) / (π·dm + μ·l·secα) + collar term. Positive means self-locking. Negative means the load back-drives, and the magnitude is the holding torque. Ball screw: TL = F·l/(2π)·(1/η − 2) + collar term.
  • Efficiency e = F·l / (2π·TR). A screw with e below 50% tends to self-lock.
  • Speed and power: v = l·N / 60, Pin = TR·ω, Pout = F·v.
  • Peak force Fpk = F + m·a. Screw inertia Js = (π/32)·ρ·L·dr⁴ and α = 2π·a / l. Motor torque = (TR(Fpk) + Js·α) / (G·ηdrive).
  • Screw stress: σ = 4Fpk / (π·dr²), τ = 16T / (π·dr³), von Mises σ′ = √(σ² + 3τ²).
  • Nut wear: bearing pressure pb = 2Fpk / (π·dm·Ln). Sliding speed vs = π·dm·N / (60·cosλ). PV = pb·vs.
  • Buckling, with r = dr/4 and end factor K: Euler Pcr = π²·E·I / (K·L)². If K·L/r is below √(2π²E/Sy), use Johnson Pcr = A·Sy·(1 − Sy·(K·L/r)² / (4π²E)).
  • Critical (whirl) speed: Ncr = (60/2π)·λn²·(dr/4)·√(E/ρ) / L², with ρ = 7850 kg/m³ and λn set by the end supports (1.875, π, 3.927 or 4.730).
  • Resolution per microstep = l / (steps × microsteps × G).
