The beam is fixed at the left wall and free at the right. Loads act downward, so the top fibre is in tension and the bottom in compression. Colour shows bending stress through the depth: blue on the neutral axis and red at the highest stress on the beam, or at yield if you switch the colour scale under Inputs. The deflection is drawn at true scale and the callout gives the actual tip drop. The Deflection drawing setting in the inputs can exaggerate it.
